# 📈 Objectives & Key Results (OKRs) & KPIs
**Role Owner**: Business Owner  
**Reporting Horizon**: Quarterly  

---

## 🎯 Q3/Q4 Strategic Objectives

### Objective 1: Drive Rapid User Growth & Community Adoption
- **KR1**: Achieve 10,000 active monthly users (MAU) within 90 days of launch.
- **KR2**: Attain a 45% 30-day retention rate for users who complete at least one toy listing.
- **KR3**: Maintain a Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) under \$2.50 via organic Instagram/TikTok growth.

### Objective 2: Maximize Exchange Volume & Engagement
- **KR1**: Reach 1,500 successful toy exchanges per month.
- **KR2**: Average user session duration $>4.5$ minutes (driven by engaging video reels).
- **KR3**: $>80\%$ positive feedback rating on completed exchanges.

### Objective 3: Validate Unit Economics & Monetization
- **KR1**: Generate \$5,000 Monthly Recurring Revenue (MRR) through featured listings and exchange fees by Q4.
- **KR2**: Maintain gross margin $>85\%$ by leveraging serverless Google Firebase & cloud architecture.
