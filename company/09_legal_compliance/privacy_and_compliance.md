# ⚖️ Legal, Data Privacy & Safety Compliance Rules
**Role Owner**: Legal & Compliance Officer  
**Scope**: Child Safety (COPPA), Data Protection (GDPR/CCPA), Platform Terms of Service  

---

## 🛡️ Child Safety & COPPA Compliance (Children's Online Privacy Protection Act)

1. **Age Requirement**: Platform is strictly for adults (Parents/Guardians age 18+) to trade toys on behalf of their children.
2. **Zero Direct Child Profiling**: No direct registration allowed for individuals under age 13.
3. **No Minors in Video Media**: Upload guidelines require that toy video reels feature toys only, avoiding visible faces of minors without explicit parental consent.

---

## 📜 Terms of Service & Exchange Guidelines

- **Peer-to-Peer Disclaimer**: Toy Promote & Exchange provides the matchmaking discovery platform; users are responsible for clean, safe condition of toys exchanged.
- **Prohibited Items**: Defective electrical toys, recalled toys (cross-referenced with CPSC recall database), items missing safety warnings.
- **Fair Trade Escalation**: If an exchanged toy arrives damaged or not as described in the video reel, users can initiate a dispute within 48 hours for admin review.
