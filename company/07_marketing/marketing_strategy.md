# 📣 Marketing & Growth Strategy
**Role Owner**: CMO / Growth Marketing Lead  
**Objective**: User Acquisition, Viral Growth Loops & Community Retention  

---

## 🚀 Growth Engine Pillars

### 1. Organic Instagram & TikTok Viral Reels Strategy
- **Format**: 15-30 second video unboxing, toy transformation, retro toy nostalgia, or parent swap stories.
- **Hashtag Strategy**: `#ToySwap`, `#ParentHacks`, `#SustainableToys`, `#ToyCollector`, `#PrelovedToys`.
- **Call-To-Action (CTA)**: "Swap this toy in 1-click on Toy Exchange! Link in bio."

### 2. Parent Community Partnerships
- Partner with local mom/dad blogger networks, parenting Facebook groups, and Montessori pre-schools for neighborhood swap events.

### 3. Referral Gamification Program
- **Give $5, Get $5**: When a user invites a friend who lists their first toy, both users get 1 free "Promoted Listing Pass".

---

## 📈 Conversion Funnel Strategy

```
  [ Awareness ]  --> Instagram/TikTok Reels & Influencer Content
        │
  [ Interest ]   --> Landing Page / App Reel Feed (Zero Login Friction)
        │
  [ Action ]     --> First Toy Swap Proposal / First Toy Upload
        │
  [ Retention ]  --> Push Notifications ("New Toy Available Near You!")
```
